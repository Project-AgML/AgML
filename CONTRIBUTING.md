# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for choosing to contribute to AgML!

Our updated contributing guidelines are located under the docs tab of the updated (project-agml.github.io)[https://project-agml.github.io/] website. 

## Contributing Data

To contribute a new dataset, please follow the guidelines listed under the (development)[https://project-agml.github.io/docs/development] tab. 

## Contributing Leaderboard Results

To add a model's benchmarking results to the leaderboard, please follow the guidelines listed under the (contributing leaderboard results)[https://project-agml.github.io/docs/contributing-results] tab.
